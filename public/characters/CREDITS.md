# Character and weapon models

All files were downloaded from Poly Pizza (https://poly.pizza). The game recolours
every model into its ink style. It removes the props some characters ship with,
because the game draws its own weapons. Models without a skeleton or animations
are rigged automatically at load time with the SWAT's skeleton and animations
(`src/game/autorig.ts`). Under CC-BY these are changes: recoloured, props
removed, and for the auto-rigged models, rigged and animated.
